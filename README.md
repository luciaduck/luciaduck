### 🐣 **Story of an ugly duckling**

I started coding when `<frameset>` was still a thing and I was in middle school, but late 2000s tech forums were too poisonous for a beginner who hated homework. My first tool was a **spreadsheet to solve proportions**. *I wasn't exactly the cool girl.*

I **dove back in recently**, when AI helped me stop worrying about syntax details and missing closing tags. *I still miss them, anyway.*

I love to tell my students that we *"learn by mistakes"* and I promise I will make a lot of them — feel free to tell me when you find one, but please be nice: *I'm Italian and I can get a bit passionate.*
